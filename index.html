YBK Synth Player

Play, Recover, Edit, Export. Your VSTs, without the DAW.

$5 One-time · One license · One device
Photo coming soon
Synth Player running an instrument

Fig. 1 — the app in use Drop a 16:9 screenshot or clip here

Synth Player is a standalone host for the VST instruments you already own. It scans your plugin folders on launch, lists what it finds, and opens any one of them on its own — full interface, keyboard input, audio out, nothing else in the way.

No empty project. No track to arm. No plugin window buried behind an arrangement view. Click the instrument, play the instrument. Don’t let DAW shenanigans get in the way of your creativity.

And because the best thing you play is almost always the thing you played before you were ready, it records continuously in the background. Every note, every bend, every held pedal, from the moment the instrument opens.

YBK MidSide

A mixing console for everything your PC plays.

$5 One-time · One license · One device
Photo coming soon
The capsule floating over a desktop

Fig. 1 — the app in use Drop a 16:9 screenshot or clip here

MidSide is a floating capsule that sits above every other window and processes your system audio in real time. Mid/Side balance, stereo width and a sweepable band-pass filter — applied to anything playing on your machine, not just to a track in a project.

Check a reference mix in mono before you commit to yours. Solo the side channel of a finished record and hear exactly what a mastering engineer put out there. Sweep a band across whatever is playing.

Close the window and it keeps running from the system tray. There is no project, no session, and nothing to save.

Width

Mono to extra wide

Scales the side signal while the mid stays intact — 0 collapses to mono, 1 is untouched, 2 widens.

Mid / Side

Solo either half of the image

Crossfade between hearing only the centre content and only the stereo difference.

Band sweep

20 Hz to 20 kHz, variable Q

A band-pass you can drag across the spectrum, with its own concentric ring for resonance.

Always available

Floats above everything

Stays on top of other windows, drags anywhere on the desktop, and keeps processing from the tray.

What you’ll need

To process system audio, MidSide needs a virtual audio device. If you don’t already use an audio interface, install the free VB-Audio Virtual Cable — the app walks you through the three-step setup on first launch.
